Lasers & Skin Rejuvenation

Lasers work on the surface of the skin, which is a different problem from the lines Botox treats. A laser treatment can soften sun damage, even out skin tone, and improve texture over time. It is a good option if your skin looks dull or uneven, or if you have spots from the sun that bother you, which is common if you work outdoors. The team will look at your skin and tell you which laser fits, what to expect, and how many sessions you are likely to need. There may be some redness for a day or two, and the team will tell you how to care for your skin afterward. Many people combine laser treatments with Botox, because one works on the surface and the other works on the muscles underneath.

Microneedling Facials & Peels

Microneedling and peels are treatments for skin texture. Microneedling uses tiny needles to create small, controlled openings in the skin, which prompts the skin to make new collagen. Over a few weeks, the skin looks smoother, pores look smaller, and old acne scars look softer. A peel removes the outer layer of dull skin, which leaves the skin looking brighter and feeling smoother. Both are good options if you want to improve your skin without injectables, and both pair well with Botox. There is some redness for a day or two, like a mild sunburn, and the team will tell you how to care for your skin afterward. A series of treatments usually gives the best result.

Injectables & PRF

PRF uses your own blood, spun down to concentrate the platelets, to support healing and skin quality. The team will talk through which injectable fits your concern, how much to use, and what it costs. XERF (Skin Tightening)

XERF is a skin tightening treatment, and it works on firmness rather than lines. As we age, the skin loses collagen and starts to look less firm, especially around the jaw and the neck. XERF uses radiofrequency energy to warm the deeper layers of the skin, which prompts the skin to make new collagen over time. The change is gradual, which is why people sometimes do not notice it day by day, and then they look at a photo from a few months earlier and see the difference. It is a good option if your skin feels loose or tired, and it pairs well with Botox, because one works on firmness and the other on the lines that movement creates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Botox for men?

Yes. More men try Botox every year, and the treatment is the same, though men often need a slightly larger dose because their muscles are stronger. The team will tell you how many units you are likely to need before they start.

Will anyone be able to tell I had Botox?

Not when it is done well. A conservative dose softens the lines while letting you move your face normally. The best compliment is that nobody can tell what changed, only that you look rested.

Does Botox hurt?

Most people feel a small pinch that lasts a second. The needles are very fine, and the treatment takes only a few minutes. If you are nervous, tell the team, and they will go slowly and talk you through each step.

How long does Botox last?

About three to four months for most people. The effect builds over a few days, peaks at about two weeks, and then fades gradually. Most clients book their next visit when they notice the lines returning.

Can I go back to work the same day?

Yes. Most people go straight back to work after Botox. Avoid lying down or rubbing the area for a few hours, and skip heavy exercise that day. The team will give you written aftercare.

What is the difference between Botox and filler?

Botox relaxes the muscles that create lines when you move your face. Filler adds volume where it has been lost, or shapes a feature like the jaw or cheeks. Many people use both, and the team will explain which one fits each concern.

How do I know if Botox is right for me?

Book a consultation. The team will look at your skin, watch how your face moves, and tell you honestly whether Botox will help, and how much you are likely to need. If nothing is needed yet, they will say so.
